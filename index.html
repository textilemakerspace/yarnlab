YarnLab

Where fiber-crafts meet the history and future of data.

YarnLab, part of Stanford's CESTA digital humanities center on the 4th floor of Wallenberg, is a friendly drop-in space centered on yarn and other fiber-crafts. We celebrate the role of fiber-crafts in the history of technology (e.g. the Jacquard loom, punch-card knitting machines), and possible futures for endangered data with methods like data visualization with textiles.

You don't have to work with data to come spend time at YarnLab! Bring your friends and your personal craft projects, and enjoy our supplies, materials, and comfy seating.

YarnLab is available on weekdays for anyone to drop in during regular business hours. We'll have a limited number of staffed hours soon along with a fall workshop schedule!

Questions? Email Quinn Daedal at qad@stanford.edu.
