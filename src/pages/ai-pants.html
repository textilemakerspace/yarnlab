“Who Wears the Pants in Education?” is a zine created by a team of 9 Stanford library workers in fall 2026 for a local “Thought Leadership in AI and Education” contest. The hand-lettered zine is a digitally-shareable representation of the team’s primary creation: a pair of hand-sewn pants with the text of a manifesto written, beaded, and hand- and machine-embroidered onto the fabric. The lining of the numerous pockets has responses to an anonymous survey of Stanford library workers about how AI has impacted their work, written atop a banana-print fabric capturing the sentiment of “bananapants”. The manifesto stakes out the position that education is an embodied experience, and struggling with new material is an essential part of becoming an expert, unattainable through the relative ease of using generative AI.

The pants are on long-term display at YarnLab. Printed copies of the zine are available intermittently (we hand-bind this stuff!) at YarnLab and outside Pigott 108.
